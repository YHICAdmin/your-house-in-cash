# Purpose

Introduce Noah as the person behind Your House in Cash and establish a clear, direct, homeowner-first approach to selling a Cleveland home.

# Page Routing

- Canonical route: `/about-us`
- The Hero primary CTA navigates to the Contact Us page at `/contact`.

# Hero

## Purpose

Create a personal first impression of Noah and make clear that Your House in Cash offers a straightforward conversation about a homeowner’s options.

## Content

### Primary Headline

- `Hi, I’m Noah. I make Cleveland home sales clearer.`
- Use H1 semantics and the global Display typography.

### Supporting Copy

- `My Cleveland real estate journey began in 2021. I believe homeowners deserve a straightforward conversation about their options—not pressure, confusing sales language, or a one-size-fits-all answer.`
- Use global Body Large typography.

### Primary CTA

- Label: `Talk Through Your Options`
- Destination: `/contact`
- Use the shared Button Component with its `conversion` variant.

## Layout

### Desktop

- Use a spacious two-column Hero.
- Use a `5 / 1 / 6` composition: the left five columns contain the Primary Headline, Supporting Copy, and Primary CTA in that order; the center column remains open; the right six columns contain one abstract branded visual.
- Keep the text composition as the Hero’s primary focal point.

### Mobile

- Stack the text composition first and the abstract visual second.
- Keep the abstract visual proportional and visually supporting the copy.

## Abstract Branded Visual

### Purpose

Provide a warm, modern branded counterweight to the Hero copy without using an image asset.

### Design Direction

- Build one rounded rectangle with CSS gradients and soft blurred color fields.
- Use pale green, soft sage, warm off-white, and deep forest green close to the existing brand palette.
- Create a polished, warm, understated color composition with the visual weight of an editorial background.
- Use the supplied reference as loose inspiration for soft-color composition only.

# Maple Heights House Rents: 2026 Snapshot and 2027 Outlook

In September 2026, advertised rents for three- and four-bedroom houses in Maple Heights averaged roughly $1,600 to $1,800 a month, according to [Apartments.com](https://www.apartments.com/rent-market-trends/maple-heights-oh/). If you own a single-family rental, this snapshot shows the house figures first, then explains recent changes in nearby rental listings and what they may mean for 2027.

## Asking rents for Maple Heights houses

These [Apartments.com house averages](https://www.apartments.com/rent-market-trends/maple-heights-oh/) were published in September 2026:

| House type | Average advertised rent per month |
| --- | --- |
| Three-bedroom house | $1,641 |
| Four-bedroom house | $1,790 |

At those asking rents, a full year with no vacancy would bring in about $19,700 for a three-bedroom house or $21,500 for a four-bedroom house, before expenses.

## What changed in the nearby ZIP codes

The broader rental figures moved in different directions. [Realtor.com's August 2026 data for 44137](https://www.realtor.com/local/market/ohio/zipcode-44137) and [44125](https://www.realtor.com/local/market/ohio/zipcode-44125) show:

| ZIP code | Median advertised rent per month | Change from a year earlier | Rentals listed |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 44137 | $1,495 | Up 10.82% | 59 |
| 44125 | $1,422 | Down 1.93% | 12 |

The 44137 ZIP is Maple Heights's main ZIP code. The 44125 ZIP [also covers Garfield Heights and other communities](https://www.zipdatamaps.com/44125), so its figure is nearby context rather than a Maple Heights-only price. Both ZIP figures include property types other than single-family houses.

## Why asking rents may have risen in 44137

[Zillow rated Maple Heights a warm rental market](https://www.zillow.com/rental-manager/market-trends/maple-heights-oh/) in September 2026, based on renter demand. In [Realtor.com's August 2026 data](https://www.realtor.com/local/market/ohio/zipcode-44137), 44137 had 59 rental listings, up 42.50% from a year earlier, while the median asking rent rose 10.82%. One plausible explanation is that renter interest supported higher asking prices despite the greater choice of rentals. Zillow's demand rating covers all property types, so it does not measure demand for houses alone.

## What to expect in 2027

Our outlook is for Maple Heights house asking rents to stay broadly steady or rise modestly in 2027, rather than repeat the 10.82% ZIP-wide increase reported for 44137. This is an inference from mixed signals: [Zillow's September 2026 citywide average](https://www.zillow.com/rental-manager/market-trends/maple-heights-oh/) across all property types was only $25 higher than a year earlier; 44125's median slipped; and 44137 had substantially more rentals listed. Those measures point to a more measured outlook than the 44137 headline alone suggests.

For an owner weighing another rental term, the practical comparison is the rent similar houses can ask when yours becomes available, alongside expected vacancy, repairs, and ongoing costs. The table gives you a place to start. When you're ready to rent the house, its condition and the other homes available nearby will help you decide what to ask.

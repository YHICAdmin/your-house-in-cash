# Maple Heights Rent Snapshot for 2026

The median advertised rent in Maple Heights's main ZIP code, 44137, reached $1,495 in August 2026, up 10.82% from a year earlier in [Realtor.com's data](https://www.realtor.com/local/market/ohio/zipcode-44137). If you own a rental house, that rise gives you a reason to look at what similar houses now ask. The neighboring 44125 ZIP moved slightly in the other direction. This snapshot separates broad rental figures from house-specific ones so you can see which numbers are useful for your property.

## Maple Heights rents at a glance

[Zillow's Maple Heights rental summary](https://www.zillow.com/rental-manager/market-trends/maple-heights-oh/), updated September 30, 2026, puts the average advertised rent at $1,650 per month across all bedroom counts and property types. Its reported year-over-year change is $25. That is a broad listing average, not a typical rent for a house or the amount existing tenants are paying.

Another common figure looks much lower: [Apartments.com reports an average of $791 per month](https://www.apartments.com/rent-market-trends/maple-heights-oh/) for Maple Heights in September 2026. That headline describes apartments, not the full mix of houses and apartments in Zillow's figure. The two numbers answer different questions and should not be treated as competing estimates for the same home.

## What changed in 44137

ZIP code 44137 is the clearest ZIP-level view of Maple Heights. In [Realtor.com's August 2026 data](https://www.realtor.com/local/market/ohio/zipcode-44137), its median advertised rent was $1,495 per month, up 10.82% from a year earlier. The site counted 59 rental properties, up 42.50% year over year. More advertised rentals alongside a higher median does not, by itself, show that demand rose: the mix of homes available can change the median.

This is a ZIP-wide, all-property-type measure. A three-bedroom house may be priced differently from an apartment, and condition, size, amenities, and lease terms all affect an individual asking rent.

## How 44125 compares

The nearby 44125 ZIP includes Garfield Heights and other communities as well as part of Maple Heights. Its numbers should not be labeled as Maple Heights-only rents. [Realtor.com's August 2026 ZIP data](https://www.realtor.com/local/market/ohio/zipcode-44125) shows a median advertised rent of $1,422 per month, down 1.93% year over year, with 12 rental properties listed. [The ZIP boundary description](https://www.zipdatamaps.com/44125) confirms that 44125 spans multiple municipalities.

The 44125 median was $73 below 44137's in that same source and month. With only 12 rentals counted in 44125, a few different listings could shift its median noticeably. The difference is useful geographic context, not a precise rent discount for an otherwise identical home across the ZIP line.

## Rent for single-family houses

For a landlord or homeowner with a detached house, apartment averages are a poor shortcut. [Apartments.com's September 2026 Maple Heights figures](https://www.apartments.com/rent-market-trends/maple-heights-oh/) show $1,641 per month for a three-bedroom house and $1,790 for a four-bedroom house. These are asking-rent averages by house size, not year-over-year trends for those house categories.

Taken together, the available data says rents in the core Maple Heights ZIP were higher than a year earlier, but it does not establish that every single-family home gained the same amount. A home's likely rent depends on comparable houses with similar bedrooms, condition, location, and included features—not on a citywide average alone.
